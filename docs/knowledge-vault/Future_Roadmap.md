# Knowledge Vault — Future Roadmap

Phased expansion of the Knowledge Vault ecosystem. Phase 1 is the active milestone on `feature/knowledge-vault`.

---

## Phase 1 — Homepage Knowledge Vault Preview

**Status:** branch ready; implementation pending

Includes:

- Research. Frameworks. Context. hero
- Statistics cards
- Knowledge Vault category cards
- Lesson preview + report reader (UI redesign only)
- *A Journey Years in the Making* section
- Responsive behaviour
- Smooth interactions and animations

Does **not** include a dedicated vault page, search, filters, bookmarks, or deep navigation.

---

## Phase 2 — Dedicated Knowledge Vault

- Dedicated Knowledge Vault experience / page
- Search
- Filters
- Categories (full archive navigation)
- Bookmarks
- Reading progress

Homepage preview remains a teaser into this destination.

---

## Phase 3 — AI Knowledge Search

- AI-assisted discovery across frameworks, reports, and intelligence
- Query → relevant lessons / reports / outlooks

---

## Phase 4 — Analyst Research Library

- Analyst publications integrated into the vault
- Library surfaces for analyst-authored research (product coupling defined later; vault remains the reader destination)

---

## Phase 5 — Community Publications

- Community contribution / publication pathways
- Curation and quality controls (product rules TBD)

Do not fold unrelated Admin, Analyst management, Member Dashboard, or Payment work into this branch.
