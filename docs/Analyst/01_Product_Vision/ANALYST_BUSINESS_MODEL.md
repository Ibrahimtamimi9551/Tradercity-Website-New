## Value exchange

| Party | Gives | Gets |
|-------|-------|------|
| **Members** | Subscription / engagement | High-quality research & education |
| **Analysts** | Research, education, audience growth | Recurring commission, brand reach, infrastructure |
| **TraderCity** | Site, payments, memberships, Discord, analytics, referrals, brand | Platform growth, retention, differentiated content |

---

## TraderCity provides (infrastructure)

Website · Payments · Memberships · Discord · Community · Analytics · Dashboards · Referral infrastructure · Commission system · Brand · Growth opportunities

## Analysts focus on

Publishing · Research · Market analysis · Educational content · Growing their audience

---

## Commission principles (product)

- Earnings derive from attributable value (referrals / conversions / agreed structures)  
- Analysts see pending / approved / lifetime earnings and tier progress (future partner dashboard)  
- Payout requests are **rate-limited** (target: 1–2 / month) and **Admin-approved**  
- Wallet / payout destination collected during **onboarding** (not application)  

Detailed Admin ops: [`../04_Admin/COMMISSION_MANAGEMENT.md`](../04_Admin/COMMISSION_MANAGEMENT.md) (planned depth).

## Evaluation economics (not vanity metrics)

Partnership decisions weigh **Credibility · Quality · Reach · Compatibility · Communication**.  
Compatibility / ethics can veto high-reach applicants. Scorecards are **internal only**.

See lifecycle evaluation in [`ANALYST_USER_LIFECYCLE.md`](./ANALYST_USER_LIFECYCLE.md).
