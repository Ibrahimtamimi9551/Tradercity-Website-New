## Intent

Public `/analysts` landing that convinces independent analysts to partner with TraderCity rather than operate alone, and ensures **every** applicant sees the same program information before applying.

CTA → `/analysts/apply`  
Full journey: [`APPLICATION_FLOW.md`](./APPLICATION_FLOW.md)

---

## Required for all entry points

The landing page is **not** optional — including when the user comes from the Free or VIP Member Dashboard.

| Entry | Path |
|-------|------|
| Homepage teaser | Homepage → `/analysts` → Apply |
| Free Dashboard card | Dashboard → `/analysts` → Apply |
| VIP Dashboard card | Dashboard → `/analysts` → Apply |

Reason: explain offers, expectations, benefits, revenue opportunities, brand building, and requirements before the form.

---

## Content pillars

- Why partner with TraderCity  
- Infrastructure benefits (brand, audience, payments, Discord, commissions)  
- Expectations and requirements  
- Revenue / brand-building opportunities  
- Focus on publishing (TraderCity handles infrastructure)  
- Clear CTA to apply
